Understanding
dysgraphia.

Dysgraphia makes writing difficult. It can affect how a learner forms letters, spaces words and puts ideas on paper.

It does not reflect a learner’s intelligence. Different learners need different kinds of support.

Learn more:NIHMedlinePlus

Signs to notice

A learner may have trouble forming letters, spelling or organizing ideas. They may tire quickly or avoid writing.

Look for difficulties that continue over time. Messy handwriting alone does not mean a learner has dysgraphia.

When to ask for help

Writing difficulties can have several causes. A qualified professional can look at the learner’s writing and learning needs.

If the difficulties continue, speak with their teacher and ask about an educational assessment.

Ways to help

Support may include writing lessons, changes to classwork or tools such as typing and speech-to-text.

Teachers, families and specialists can choose support together and check how it is working.

Questions
& answers.

Can GraphiScan diagnose dysgraphia?

No. GraphiScan supports handwriting screening and expert review. It does not replace a professional assessment.

Who can use GraphiScan?

Teachers, experts and linked parents use the mobile app. Administrators use the web workspace. The project focuses on Grades 1–3. You need an account to sign in.

What can parents and guardians see?

Parents can read reports only for children linked to their account, and only while the expert review is marked Validated.

Is the Android app available?

The Android app is not ready to download yet. When a release is available, the download page will show the link and installation steps.

How is learner information handled?

The app uses sign-in, record-access checks and private image storage. This website shows example records and has no learner upload form. Read the data-use information for the current scope.
