About GraphiScan.

GraphiScan helps teachers, experts and families keep track of handwriting screenings and reviewed reports.

Project purpose

The project focuses on learners in Grades 1–3. It brings handwriting samples, screening information and expert notes into one shared record.

Who uses GraphiScan?

  • TeachersUpload handwriting for assigned learners.
  • ExpertsReview assigned screenings and add notes.
  • Parents & guardiansRead reports for linked children while the review is marked Validated.
  • AdministratorsManage accounts, learners and assignments on the web.

Screening and review

The model returns either Normal or High Potential. An expert records a separate review. A model label or score is not a diagnosis.

Application previews

The pictures on this website show real GraphiScan screens with made-up example records. They do not show private learner information or prove clinical effectiveness.
