## Proposed notice and account rules

### 1. About GraphiScan

GraphiScan supports handwriting screening, expert review and follow-up for learners in Grades 1 to 3. It is a BSIT capstone project developed at Davao del Norte State College, Institute of Computing. The organization responsible for this service is **[RESPONSIBLE ORGANIZATION]**. For privacy questions or requests, contact **[PRIVACY CONTACT]**.

### 2. Information handled and why

Depending on your role, the service handles account and role information; school and student details; teacher and parent or guardian links; handwriting images; model screening results; expert reviews and recommendations; report history; and relevant account or activity records.

The purposes are account administration, authorized screening, expert review, reporting, follow-up and system accountability. The lawful basis for each purpose is **[CONFIRMED PROCESSING BASIS]**. Data will not be used for unrelated research, model training or publicity merely because an account holder acknowledges this notice. Any additional purpose requires its own appropriate basis and any required information and permissions.

### 3. Who can access information

Access depends on the user's role, assignments and relationship to the record. Teachers work with their assigned students; experts review screenings within their authorized scope; parents or guardians access released information for linked children; and authorized administrators manage the service within their permissions.

An individual screening report is available to a linked parent when it is currently Validated. A current final summary and conclusion can include the full reviewed history, including Flagged screenings. **[VERIFY AGAINST THE VERSION ACTUALLY DEPLOYED BEFORE PUBLICATION.]**

The service uses **[CONFIRMED AUTHENTICATION, HOSTING, DATABASE AND STORAGE PROVIDERS]**. Information may be disclosed to **[CONFIRMED RECIPIENTS AND PURPOSES]** and processed in **[CONFIRMED LOCATIONS / CROSS-BORDER DETAILS]**. Access is limited through account and record permissions.

### 4. Retention and requests

Information is retained for **[CONFIRMED PERIODS FOR EACH RELEVANT RECORD TYPE]**. After those periods, **[CONFIRMED DELETION OR OTHER DISPOSITION]** applies. Disabling or deleting an account does not necessarily remove all related student records or audit history.

To ask about your information or exercise applicable rights, including access or correction, contact **[PRIVACY CONTACT AND REQUEST PROCEDURE]**. **[ADD VERIFIED PROCEDURE FOR REQUESTS, OBJECTIONS, WITHDRAWAL WHEN APPLICABLE, AND COMPLAINTS.]**

### 5. Screening and student participation

The model produces screening labels and scores for expert review. They are not a diagnosis or a clinical probability and must not be the sole basis for a consequential decision about a learner. Expert observations and recommendations are attributed to the expert.

Before submitting a student's information or handwriting, follow **[CONFIRMED SCHOOL AUTHORIZATION AND PARTICIPATION PROCESS]**. A user acknowledging this account notice does not by itself establish a child's or guardian's permission or another lawful basis for processing the child's data.

### 6. Rules for account use

Use your own account, keep your password private, submit only information you are authorized to use, and access or share records only as permitted for your role. Report suspected unauthorized access to **[CONFIRMED SECURITY CONTACT]**.

### 7. Acknowledgement

Please read the notice and account rules before continuing. By selecting the acknowledgement control and continuing, you confirm that you have read them and agree to follow the account-use rules. GraphiScan records the notice version and time of acknowledgement. If the notice changes, the service may ask you to review a new version. You may sign out without continuing.

This acknowledgement is not a blanket consent for every processing purpose or a substitute for a child's participation permission. Any consent that is actually needed must be separately designed for its purpose and reviewed with the responsible organization.
