1. About GraphiScan

GraphiScan supports handwriting screening, expert review and progress monitoring for elementary learners in Grades 1 to 3. It is a BSIT capstone project developed at Davao del Norte State College, Institute of Computing. This notice explains the information handled by the application and your responsibilities when using it.

2. Information we handle

GraphiScan handles account details and roles, school and student information, teacher and parent or guardian links, handwriting images, screening results, model information, expert remarks and recommendations, report history, and relevant account and activity records.

This information supports account administration, authorized handwriting screening, expert review, report access and follow-up monitoring. Submit only information needed for these purposes and which you are authorized to provide. Accepting this notice does not authorize unrelated research, model training, publicity or external sharing.

3. Access to records

Access depends on the account's role, assigned permissions and relationship to each record. Teachers work with their assigned students. Experts review screenings assigned to them. Parents and guardians can view released reports for their linked children while the expert review is marked Validated. Administrators and authorized researchers perform management tasks within their granted permissions.

Keep your password private, use your own account and do not access or disclose records without authorization. Report suspected unauthorized access to the GraphiScan administrator who issued your account.

4. Storage and account history

GraphiScan uses Supabase Auth for authentication, PostgreSQL for application records and private Storage for handwriting images. The API checks account status, permissions and record relationships before providing protected information. These controls restrict access but do not eliminate every security risk.

Account activity and administrative actions may be recorded for accountability. Disabling an account blocks access while preserving linked records and history. Deleting an account does not automatically erase all related records or audit logs.

5. Responsible screening and student participation

Automated screening labels and scores are indicators for review, not a diagnosis. They must not be presented as a medical conclusion or used as the sole basis for a consequential decision about a learner. Expert remarks and recommendations are attributed to the reviewing expert.

Before submitting student information or handwriting, obtain the required school authorization and parent or guardian permission. Acceptance of this account notice does not replace permission for a child's participation.

6. Questions and record requests

For questions about your information, corrections, retention or removal of records, contact the GraphiScan project team through the administrator who issued your account. Requests require review of the affected records and applicable responsibilities. This notice does not promise an automatic deletion schedule or immediate removal of linked history.

7. Your acknowledgement

By selecting Accept and continue, you acknowledge that you have read this notice and agree to follow the stated account-use and confidentiality responsibilities. GraphiScan records the accepted notice version and the server-recorded acceptance time. If you choose not to accept, sign out without continuing to protected features.

A newly published notice requires a new acknowledgement. Earlier acceptances remain associated with their original versions.
