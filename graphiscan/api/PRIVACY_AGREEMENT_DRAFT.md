# Proposed GraphiScan privacy agreement

Draft prepared for the progress-report screen preview. Unpublished; it does not replace either hosted test notice or authorize real student-data processing. Institutional review must supply the responsible organization and privacy contact, lawful basis, participation/guardian process, retention periods, recipients/providers and hosting details before approval. Version 1.0 below is proposed display text, not a published policy version.

References: [NPC right to be informed](https://privacy.gov.ph/the-right-to-be-informed/), [Data Privacy Act](https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act/). These references do not establish compliance or approval.

1. Purpose of GraphiScan

GraphiScan supports handwriting screening, expert review, progress monitoring, and reporting. Automated results are screening indicators for professional review. They do not establish a medical diagnosis or replace a qualified professional's assessment.

2. Information handled

System records may include account and role information, linked student and school details, handwriting images, screening outputs, model identifiers, expert reviews, recommendations, report history, and activity records. Submit only information needed for an authorized screening or review.

3. Permitted use of information

Use information only for authorized screening, review, progress monitoring, reporting, and system administration. Unrelated research, model training, publicity, or external sharing requires separate authorization and an applicable basis. Accepting this agreement alone does not authorize those activities.

4. Access and confidentiality

Access depends on the user's assigned role, permissions, and relationship to the record. Users must protect account credentials and keep student information confidential. Do not access, copy, download, or disclose another person's records without authority.

5. Responsible use

Provide accurate information, upload only material you are authorized to submit, and report suspected unauthorized access to your institution's designated administrator. Do not share accounts, misrepresent a screening result, or use automated classifications as the sole basis for a consequential decision about a student.

6. Student and guardian participation

The responsible institution must establish the appropriate school and parent or guardian permissions before processing student records. An adult user's acceptance of this account agreement does not replace any permission required for a child's participation.

7. Retention and privacy requests

Personal records must be retained only for the declared purposes or applicable recordkeeping obligations. Requests about access, correction, objection, restriction, or deletion should be directed to the responsible institution through its designated privacy contact. Applicable conditions and recordkeeping requirements may affect a request.

8. Agreement updates

GraphiScan records the version accepted by an account. Review any updated agreement presented before continuing to protected system features. The responsible institution must provide its identity, privacy contact, retention periods, and relevant processing arrangements before collecting actual student records.
